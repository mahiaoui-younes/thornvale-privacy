Privacy Policy

Thornvale: Tower Defense (com.thornvale.lastdefense)
Effective date: November 2, 2026

This policy explains how the game Thornvale: Tower Defense ("the game") handles your information. In short: the game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it has no account or sign-in.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (completed levels, stars, best results) and your settings (music and sound volume, difficulty, tips) on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the game's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

Internet, ads and analytics

The game does not connect to the internet. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party services that collect data.

Permissions

The game does not request any special device permissions.

Sharing of information

Because no personal data is collected, none is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
